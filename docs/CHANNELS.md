# CHANNELS — the five legitimate vectors

3-5 channels max per story. Same story, different legitimate vectors. Each
channel entry below lists: the angle, where it lives, and what "legitimate"
means there (the rule that keeps us out of spam).

## 1. Journalism angle
- **Where:** felonsmelons.com, Muck Rack byline pitches, local Utah outlets
  (Salt Lake Tribune, Deseret News, Utah Valley papers), Substack/newsletter
  cross-posts.
- **Angle:** the story as reporting — who is affected, what the data says.
- **Legitimacy rule:** it reads as journalism first. If it couldn't run
  somewhere with no connection to us, it isn't ready.

## 2. Employer angle
- **Where:** LinkedIn posts + direct packets to second-chance employers,
  staffing agencies, HR groups already discussing hiring shortages.
- **Angle:** what the labor data means for an employer with unfilled roles.
- **Legitimacy rule:** the packet is useful on its own (stats, contacts,
  program lists) even if they never visit the site.

## 3. Reentry angle
- **Where:** reentry org newsletters, prison newsletters, workforce program
  boards, DWS-adjacent channels, Facebook/Reddit reentry groups (as a
  participant, not an advertiser).
- **Angle:** practical navigation — what exists, what's missing, how to use it.
- **Legitimacy rule:** written for the people living it, not about them.
  No open-case anything.

## 4. Economics angle
- **Where:** local economic reporting comments/letters, workforce-development
  discussions, chamber / economic-dev newsletters, policy briefs.
- **Angle:** the numbers — labor shortage costs, recidivism costs, wage
  uplift from second-chance hiring.
- **Legitimacy rule:** every number sourced or flagged. No vibes.

## 5. Lived-experience angle
- **Where:** Kirk's own channels — LinkedIn, the gazette, Felons Melon
  mission pieces, podcast/newsletter appearances.
- **Angle:** first-person reality of reentry in Utah. Parole, sobriety,
  neurodivergence, the paperwork treadmill.
- **Legitimacy rule:** Kirk's voice only, Kirk's lived experience only, and
  Kirk decides what part of his story is public. We never speak his life
  without him.

## Channel discipline
- Max 3-5 of these per story, chosen in step 07 and recorded in
  `07-distribution.md`.
- Never all five at once on day one of a story — pick 3, watch signal, add
  vectors only when the contrast (step 09) says the story carries.
- Every channel touch gets a SIGNAL-LOG row (step 08) — even silence. Silence
  is data.
