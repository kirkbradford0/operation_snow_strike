## The funnel (with honest placeholder rates)

```
Outreach packets sent (Kirk clicks)          5/day x 95 days        = ~475
  -> reply rate            (assume 10%)                             ~47 conversations
Cited/shared/mentioned touches             (from distribute step)   uncounted bonus
Site visits from all vectors               (assume 3-5x conversations + organic)
  -> visit-to-signup       (assume 5%)                              = signups
  -> signup-to-ACTIVE-user (assume 40%)                             = ACTIVE USERS
```

**Worked example at placeholder rates:**
- 475 packets -> 47 replies -> ~235 site visits from outreach alone
- Distribution channels add organic visits (assume 2x outreach traffic) ~470
- 700 visits x 5% signup = 35 signups — **not enough**

**So the story traffic has to do the heavy lifting.** 1 story/day x 95 days =
95 substantive pieces. Journalism that gets cited travels further than any
email we send. Two or three pieces landing in a reentry org newsletter or a
local outlet does in one day what a week of packets does.

## Three levers (in priority order)

1. **Story quality.** The byline-courtesy model only works if the story is
   worth sharing on its own. This is why the loop makes REPORT the core
   artifact and VERIFY a separate seat.
2. **Reply conversion.** If packets are genuinely useful (not pitches),
   reply rates run 2-5x normal cold outreach. Useful packet = the moat.
3. **Reactivation.** Every contact who ever replied is a warm node. Nov 16+,
   the contrast step shifts targets from "new contacts" to "warm nodes" —
   new stories worth sending to people who already opened the last one.

## What 100 needs (sanity check)

- ~100 active users = ~250 signups = ~5,000 qualified site visits over 95 days
  = ~53 qualified visits/day.
- 5 packets/day (Kirk sends) + 1 story/day + 3-5 channel touches/day is the
  minimum steady-state load to plausibly hit that. Any day the loop doesn't
  fire, the daily deficit is ~53 visits.
- The daily output table IS the funnel plan:
  10-20 discoveries -> 1 story -> 3-5 packets -> 3-5 channels -> measured signal
  -> better targets tomorrow.
