# TEAM.md — The Five Seats

We run this like a five-person SaaS marketing team. In practice the seats are
hats: any agent (or Kirk) wears one at a time, and the loop rotates through
all five every day. One job, then rotate.

| # | Seat | Owns | Loop steps | Daily deliverable |
|---|------|------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | **EDITOR-IN-CHIEF** | The loop itself. Sequence discipline, contrast, next-volley targeting. | 9, 10 | `09-contrast.md`, `10-next-volley.md`, volley folder committed & pushed |
| 2 | **INTELLIGENCE ANALYST** | Scraping public sources, spotting narrative raw material, gap analysis. | 1, 2 | `01-scrape.md` (10-20 discoveries), `02-narrative.md` |
| 3 | **STAFF JOURNALIST** | The story. Reports in Kirk's voice, drafts, revises, formats. | 4 | `04-report.md` — one substantive piece |
| 4 | **GROWTH LEAD** | Contacts, packets, channels. Useful-not-pitchy. Logs every send & reply. | 5, 6, 7 | `05-outreach.md`, `06-publish.md`, `07-distribution.md`, CONTACTS.md rows |
| 5 | **FACT-CHECKER / ANALYTICS** | Verification before anything moves. Signal vs noise after. | 3, 8 | `03-verify.md`, `08-observe.md`, SIGNAL-LOG.md updates |

## Seat rules

- The seat doing the verifying is never the seat that wrote the claim
  (builder never certifies own work — same doctrine as the swarm).
- Growth Lead stages packets; **Kirk sends**. Every logged response flows back
  through seat 5 before it becomes "signal."
- Editor-in-Chief ends the day with one question answered in
  `10-next-volley.md`: **"What did nobody else check?"**
- Any seat can kill a story with one word: `[UNVERIFIED-HOT]`. Nobody overrides.
